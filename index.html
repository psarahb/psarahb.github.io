Selected work

AI enablement

Finding the source of change-request rejections with AI

I used Citi's Stylus AI to analyze rejection comments across thousands of architecture change requests. One small team of 2–3 people turned out to be behind about 90% of rejections, which pointed the fix at one group instead of a firm-wide process change.

Result: a targeted fix instead of an organization-wide one
Process automation

Automated approval emails with escalation

I built weekly approval emails for each architect, with direct links to their pending items and an escalation after one week. After a committee review, architect behavior changed and turnaround on approvals improved.

Result: measurable change in approval behavior
Data governance & controls

Owning a firm-wide model-attestation control

I owned the control requiring every architecture model to be attested each year. I defended it with Audit and Operational Risk Management and kept weekly evidence. I also helped build an orphan/impact check into the new modeling tool so that undocumented applications couldn't be retired.

Result: over a year with no audit issues
Reporting & analytics

EUC compliance reporting in MicroStrategy

I taught myself MicroStrategy and built the End-User Computing compliance reports: RAG status, risk scores, counts by area, and new versus retired EUCs. I worked in an Agile Scrum setup, writing PBIs and testing through UAT and production.

Result: reporting cycle cut from one week to one day; the reports are still in use
Data management

Enterprise Data Maturity Assessment

As VP of Data Risk, I ran the baseline maturity assessment across business segments and functions using DMBOK's 11 knowledge areas and CMMI. I set the scope and criteria, ran stakeholder kickoffs, did the scoring, and set up reassessment every 6–12 months.

Result: a repeatable maturity baseline across the firm
Impact analysis

Impact reporting database

I built an impact reporting database to analyze the effects of retiring applications, capabilities, models and taxonomies. I validated data several times a week across 1,200+ models, and leadership reporting showed change-request volume, completion time and rejection trends.

Result: leadership could see where change was getting stuck
